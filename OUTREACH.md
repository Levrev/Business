## STEP 1 — Reconnaissance & architecture

### 1. Bond Sushi
- **Demographic:** office/trade workers and students on a lunch break (Mon–Fri daytime only), Varsity Lakes residents wanting cheap, quick takeaway.
- **Palette:** black `#0e0e0e`, white `#fafaf7`, signal red `#d7261e` (hinomaru nod) — minimalist Japanese.
- **Address/hours:** 201 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes QLD 4227 · Mon–Fri 10am–4pm · Sat–Sun closed.
- **USP (hero):** *Fresh sushi and proper rice bowls made daily on Varsity Parade — a great-value lunch you can pre-order and grab in minutes.*

### 2. Old Paris
- **Demographic:** early-start tradies/commuters (opens 5:30am), brunch couples and families, weekend crêpe crowd; people who want an "escape to France" without leaving the Gold Coast.
- **Palette:** cream `#f7f0e3`, French navy `#1d2a44`, awning red `#b8322a`, butter `#f1d38a` — striped-awning café.
- **Address/hours:** Shop 7, 228 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes QLD 4227 · Mon–Fri 5:30am–3pm · Sat 6am–12pm · Sun closed.
- **USP (hero):** *A family-run corner of Paris on the Gold Coast — hand-folded crêpes, handmade almond croissants and fresh baguettes from 5:30am.*

### 3. Dear Deer Milk Tea
- **Demographic:** 14–30 year-olds (students, Bond/Griffith crowd, friend groups), phone-first, Instagram/Facebook-driven; frequent delivery-app users.
- **Palette:** milk `#fff8f2`, blush `#ffd9e2`, brown-sugar `#6a3d2a`, pop pink `#ff7b9c`, taro/matcha pastels.
- **Address/hours:** A8/201 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes QLD 4227 · typically 11am–7pm (confirm).
- **USP (hero):** *Fresh-brewed milk tea, fruit tea and mochi — build your drink in seconds and have it ready when you walk in.*

### 4. Panda Noodle Bowl
- **Demographic:** lunch-break workers, families, students, delivery-app orderers who want filling Asian-fusion noodles fast.
- **Palette:** charcoal `#141414`, chilli red `#e63a1f`, noodle yellow `#ffc933`, white — loud, high-contrast, appetite-triggering.
- **Address/hours:** Shop B2, 201 Varsity Parade, Varsity Lakes QLD 4227 · hours TBC (page says "call for today's hours").
- **USP (hero):** *Big, steaming bowls of laksa, pad thai and stir-fried noodles — made to order and in your hands in minutes.*

**What each page contains:** Hero · About/Our Story (Panda: "Why you'll be back" instead) · Menu highlights · Inquiry/booking form (Netlify Forms) · Footer with contact details + disclaimer. Extras: Bond = pickup pre-order; Old Paris = table booking/pre-order/catering; Dear Deer = live drink builder + sticky mobile action bar; Panda = add-to-order cart that fills the order form + sticky Order/Call bar.
