Our story

One good coffee can change a day.

Milestone was started by Selena Mun, born and raised in Korea, with a simple belief: a single cup of coffee can become a small milestone in someone’s day.

The result is a warm, calm Southport café where Seoul-style croffles, matcha and carefully poured flat whites meet an easy mid-century setting — perfect for a slow morning, a catch-up or a little celebration.
