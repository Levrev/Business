Our Story

Small shop. Big on freshness.

Bond Sushi is a compact, no-fuss sushi shop in the heart of Varsity Lakes. We keep things simple: quality ingredients, generous portions and food made fresh through the day, so lunch is quick, filling and easy on the wallet.

Locals come back for the chicken avocado don, the katsu and a sushi roll or two for the walk back to the office. Pop in, order, grab it and go — or pre-order online so it’s ready when you arrive.
