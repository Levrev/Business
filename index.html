Our Story 物語

Cooked by the family that owns it

Tucked into a quiet arcade off Cavill Avenue, You Japanese has been serving home-style Japanese food since 1994 — long enough to become the kind of place regulars simply call "our Japanese."

Head chef and owner Satoshi Ono trained as a chef in Japan for ten years and holds a nationally recognised cooking-lecturer qualification. He runs the open kitchen alongside his wife Chiyomi, with their son Yusuke on the floor, joined by Arisa, Chinami and Auntie Mitsuko — a genuinely family-run kitchen, not a franchise wearing the label.

It shows in the room: an open servery so you can watch dinner being made, and a dining room that, on any given night, is full of Japanese guests eating alongside the regulars — usually a good sign.

The Izakaya Experience 居酒屋

What "izakaya" actually means

居酒屋 i–zaka–ya — literally "stay, sake, shop." Not a restaurant you visit once and leave — a place you settle into for the evening.

An izakaya isn't a starter-then-main kind of meal. It's small plates, ordered in rounds, meant to be shared across the table and matched with a cold beer, sake or your own bottle of wine. Order a little, eat, order a little more — there's no set course, just a night that runs at your own pace.

How To Order ご注文

Order from the tablet, whenever you're ready

  • 1Sit down, grab the tablet

    Every table orders from its own tablet — available in multiple languages, no waving down a waiter.

  • 2Order a drink and a couple of small plates

    Start light. Beer, sake, or your own BYO bottle, alongside two or three dishes to open with.

  • 3Repeat, at your own pace

    More food, more drinks, whenever the table's ready — sent straight to the kitchen the moment you order.
