Our story

Comfort food, the Korean way.

Assah (앗싸) is a Korean exclamation of joy — the little cheer you let out when something good arrives at the table. That’s the feeling we cook for.

From a steaming stone-pot stew on a cold night to a quick katsu curry or teriyaki don at lunch, our menu is built around honest, flavourful food made with care and served with banchan.

한

Authentic flavours

Stews like yukgaejang and soybean paste, plus bibimbap and bulgogi.

Lunch & dinner

Open for both services, with combos and rice dishes for a quick bite.

집

Dine in or take home

Sit down in Southport, grab takeaway, or order delivery.
