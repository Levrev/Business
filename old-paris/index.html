Notre histoire

A family kitchen with a French accent.

Old Paris is a family-run French-style café on Varsity Parade, run by owners Janet and Alex. Everything is about simple, honest food done properly — sweet and savoury crêpes folded to order, croissants and pastries baked by hand, and baguettes filled generously.

Whether you’re grabbing a coffee and croissant on the way to work or settling in for a long, lazy crêpe on Saturday morning, you’ll be looked after like family.

— Janet & Alex
