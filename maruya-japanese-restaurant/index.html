About

Authentic food. A very Japanese dining room.

Maruya (丸屋) brings classic Japanese cooking to the heart of Southport: precise sashimi, sizzling wagyu, comforting soba and rolls made to order.

Look up and you’ll spot shelves of mecha figures and movie posters — a playful, nostalgic backdrop that has become part of the Maruya experience.
