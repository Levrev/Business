Our story

Two food cultures. One good table.

Hazel Espresso has been welcoming Southport guests since November 2021, blending Australian brunch traditions with the flavours of Southeast Asia.

Expect familiar comforts — eggs, toast, a proper flat white — reimagined with Vietnamese coffee, colourful banh mi and a few surprises, in a relaxed, friendly space.
